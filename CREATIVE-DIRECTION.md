# SavvySphere — Multi-page creative direction

## Project intent

A complete, multi-page UK property consultancy website, not a single-page landing page. The experience combines HorizonX-inspired visual sophistication with ARK Consultancy's professional credibility and Purplebricks' accessible, customer-focused property journeys.

## Proposed sitemap

1. Home — `/`
2. About SavvySphere — `/about`
3. Our Services — `/services`
4. Find a Property — `/find-a-property`
5. How It Works — `/how-it-works`
6. Insights — `/insights`
   - Individual editorial pages — `/insights/[slug]`
7. Contact Us — `/contact`
8. Privacy Notice — `/privacy`
9. Terms & Conditions — `/terms`

The sitemap follows the earlier proposed structure. The Find a Property route and service categories remain provisional because the client discovery questionnaire has not been answered in the available project context. They must be confirmed or removed before launch.

## Visual system

- Deep Aubergine: `#432449`
- Muted Purple: `#745681`
- Soft Lavender: `#EAE2EE`
- Warm Ivory: `#F8F7F4`
- Deep Charcoal: `#26242A`
- Muted Grey: `#77737A`

The design uses editorial serif headings, restrained sans-serif body typography, generous whitespace, asymmetric photography, cinematic image reveals and purposeful page-to-page consistency.

## Motion and interaction

Framer Motion handles masked image reveals, staggered typography, section entrances and interactive transitions. CSS handles hover and focus states. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. No unnecessary WebGL, Three.js or excessive glassmorphism is used.

## Content integrity and launch requirements

- The service areas are conceptual placeholders until confirmed by the client.
- Find a Property is a proposed enquiry journey only; it has no live listing search, property database or inventory.
- No property listings, prices, availability, agency relationships or property-management claims are fabricated.
- Testimonials are not invented.
- Insight articles are editorial drafts for client review, not approved company publications.
- The founder story, team, company credentials, service geography and contact details must be supplied by the client.
- Privacy and Terms pages are structural placeholders, not publishable legal notices.
- Contact and property brief forms are previews only. No data is transmitted or stored. A verified CRM, email or form endpoint must be connected before launch.
- All architectural imagery is illustrative Unsplash photography and must be replaced or licensed/approved for production.
- The removed tagline “Govern / Trust / Transform” is not used.
